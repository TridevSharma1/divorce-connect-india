Billing & Payments

How are my consultation fees held in escrow?

When you engage a lawyer through DivorceConnect India, your consultation fees are securely held in our trust escrow account until the consultation is complete. This protects both you and the lawyer, ensuring payment is only released once the agreed services have been rendered. You'll receive a receipt and transaction confirmation via email within 24 hours.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major payment methods including credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), debit cards, UPI, net banking, and digital wallets. All transactions are encrypted using industry-standard SSL 256-bit encryption for your security. We do not store your full card details on our servers.

Can I get an invoice or receipt for my payment?

Yes, a detailed invoice is automatically generated and sent to your registered email address within 24 hours of payment. You can also download invoices directly from your Account Dashboard under the "Billing History" section. Invoices are issued in compliance with GST regulations and include all transaction details.

How to Hire a Lawyer

How do I securely upload documents to my lawyer?

All document uploads on DivorceConnect India are encrypted end-to-end. Navigate to your case workspace, click "Upload Documents," select files from your device (PDF, Word, images, and ZIP files supported), and they are immediately encrypted before transmission. Your lawyer receives a secure link to view and download files. Original documents remain on our encrypted servers for compliance and record-keeping.

What qualifications do DivorceConnect India lawyers have?

All lawyers on our platform are verified and licensed to practice in India. Each profile displays their Bar Council registration, years of experience, area of specialization, educational credentials, and verified client reviews. We manually verify every lawyer's credentials before they join our network to ensure the highest professional standards.

How do I schedule a consultation with a lawyer?

Browse lawyer profiles, select one that matches your needs, and click "Schedule Consultation." Choose your preferred date and time from their available slots, and the lawyer will receive a booking request. You'll be notified once they confirm. Consultations are conducted via secure video call, phone, or email—choose what suits you best.

Account Settings

How do I update my personal information?

Log into your account and navigate to "Account Settings" > "Personal Information." Update your name, contact number, email address, and address as needed. Click "Save Changes" to confirm. All changes are logged for security purposes, and you'll receive a confirmation email. Some fields like registered email require verification for security.

How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?

Go to "Account Settings" > "Security" and toggle "Enable Two-Factor Authentication." Choose your preferred method: SMS OTP or authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator). Follow the setup wizard to scan the QR code or enter the secret key. Store your backup codes in a safe place—you'll need them if you lose access to your 2FA device.

How do I delete my account?

Account deletion is available under "Account Settings" > "Advanced Options" > "Delete Account." Please note: deleted accounts cannot be recovered, and all associated case data will be permanently removed after 90 days. If you have active legal engagements, please contact our support team before deletion. A confirmation email will be sent before the deletion becomes irreversible.

Privacy Rules

How is my personal data protected?

DivorceConnect India employs enterprise-grade security measures including AES 256-bit encryption for data at rest, TLS 1.3 encryption for data in transit, regular security audits, and compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, and Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. All servers are hosted in India with secure access controls and multi-factor authentication for staff.

Will my case details be shared with third parties?

No. Your case details are shared only with the lawyer you hire, as necessary to provide legal services. We will never sell, rent, or lease your data to third parties. Sharing only occurs when legally required (court orders, regulatory compliance) or with your explicit written consent. Your communication with lawyers is protected by attorney-client privilege.

Do you comply with Indian data protection laws?

Yes, DivorceConnect India is fully compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), Information Technology Act, 2000, and relevant Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines for payment processing. We maintain detailed data processing agreements, conduct regular compliance audits, and provide transparent privacy notices. Our Data Protection Officer can be contacted at privacy@divorceconnectindia.com.
