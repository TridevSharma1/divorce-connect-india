1. Case Created
2. Document Verification

5. Petition Filed

7. Second Motion
8. Decree Issued
9. Completed

Clients bear the sole responsibility for submitting authentic documents and details at Stage 2. Lawyers retain exclusive administrative authority to update tracking phases as the dispute proceeds in the respective family court.
