Navigating Amicable Settlements: The Power of Mediation

Contributed by Adv. Rajeshwari Sen (Mumbai Bar Council)

Published on January 16, 2026 ← Back to Blog

Court trials in family disputes can drag on for years, depleting financial savings and causing immense psychological strain. In this article, I discuss how we successfully resolved a complex custody and alimony dispute within 90 days.

The Case Background

The clients had been married for 8 years and had a 6-year-old daughter. Over time, irreconcilable differences led them to seek separation. Initially, both parties were highly adversarial, claiming sole custody of the child and contesting maintenance figures.

Why Mediation Was Selected

Rather than initiating a lengthy trial in the Mumbai Family Court, I advised our client to propose structural mediation. Mediation is a confidential process facilitated by a neutral third party that focuses on finding mutually acceptable solutions rather than assigning blame.

Key Outcoms achieved:

  • Co-Parenting Plan: Agreed on joint legal custody with shared physical residency during summer and winter vacations.
  • Structured Maintenance: Reached an agreement on a one-time alimony lump sum, backed by a trust fund designated exclusively for the child's higher education.
  • Expedited Decree: Filed a petition for Mutual Consent Divorce, resulting in the decree being granted swiftly upon completion of the statutory 6-month cooling period.

Conclusion

Mediation shifts the focus from winning a battle to designing a stable future. For spouses seeking separation, prioritizing mediation can protect children from adversarial custody battles and preserve personal resources.
