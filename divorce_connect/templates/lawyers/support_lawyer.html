Frequently Asked Questions

How do platform fees and payouts work?

DivorceConnect India operates on a transparent commission model:

  • Commission Structure: 15% per consultation (deducted from client payments)
  • Escrow System: Client payments are held in secure escrow for 48 hours post-consultation to ensure quality and dispute resolution
  • Automated Clearing: After the 48-hour period, funds are automatically transferred to your registered bank account
  • Withdrawal: Payouts occur weekly on Fridays. Track all transactions in your Finance Dashboard
  • Invoice & Tax: Detailed invoices are generated for every payout for your records
How do I handle a no-show client?

Our platform protects your time with clear no-show policies:

  • 15-Minute Grace Period: Clients have 15 minutes from the scheduled time to join
  • Auto-Cancellation: After 15 minutes, the consultation is automatically marked as "No-Show"
  • Payment Held: The consultation fee remains in escrow pending resolution
  • Dispute Process: File a dispute claim with screenshots of wait time evidence
  • Resolution Timeline: Support team reviews within 4 hours; approved claims are settled within 48 hours
How do I update my legal credentials or Bar Council ID?

Keeping your credentials current is essential for trust and compliance:

  • Profile Settings: Go to Settings → Professional Credentials
  • Upload Documents: Re-upload your latest Bar Council registration certificate or practice license
  • Verification Queue: Updated documents are reviewed by our compliance team within 24 hours
  • Active Status: Your profile remains active during verification unless compliance issues arise
  • Confirmation: Email notification received once credentials are verified
How do I pause my profile if I'm on vacation?

Take a break without losing your professional standing:

  • Pause Feature: Navigate to Profile Settings → Availability
  • Set Dates: Select your vacation start and end dates
  • Client Visibility: Your profile is hidden from client search during this period
  • No Penalties: Pausing doesn't affect your ratings, reviews, or platform standing
  • Auto-Resume: Your profile automatically goes live on your return date
